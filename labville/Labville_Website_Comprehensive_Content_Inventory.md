**Delayed Diagnosis**\
The site says people may postpone seeking medical help because they are
unsure about symptoms. It states that early diagnosis is essential for
effective treatment and better outcomes, and positions Labville as a
service that can help.

**Accessible Testing**\
The site says reliable diagnostic tests can be difficult to access,
particularly where people face financial limitations. It says Labville
is committed to affordable services while maintaining high-quality
standards.

**Lack of Information**\
The site says patients may feel uncertain or confused about procedures
and results. It presents education and transparency as important for
informed health decisions.

**Healthcare Equity**\
The site says some communities face barriers to quality healthcare,
leading to health disparities. It says Labville aims to help close these
gaps through accessibility and affordability.

## 12. Visual and technical recommendations

These are design recommendations, not claims about the existing website:

-   Use a clean, modern, trustworthy healthcare visual style with
    readable typography, generous spacing and clear information
    hierarchy.
-   A restrained 3D visual language can be used for abstract,
    diagnostic-inspired elements, but it should not obstruct service
    information or contact actions.
-   Use images that accurately represent diagnostic care. Do not imply
    stock images depict Labville's actual premises, staff or equipment.
-   Make the site responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop.
-   Provide keyboard navigation, visible focus states, accessible form
    labels and sufficient colour contrast.
-   Optimise images and loading performance.
-   Keep the phone number easy to find on mobile.
-   Use descriptive page titles, metadata, semantic headings and
    meaningful image alt text.
-   Avoid distracting motion and respect reduced-motion preferences.
-   Do not create an online booking workflow, results portal or patient
    account system unless Labville explicitly approves and the feature
    is implemented end-to-end.

### Contact form requirements

-   Validate required fields and email format.
-   Provide accessible, specific error messages.
-   Prevent accidental duplicate submissions.
-   Use appropriate spam protection.
-   Keep API secrets out of frontend code.
-   Do not claim a message was delivered until the backend or form
    provider confirms it.
-   Confirm the recipient and handling process for enquiries before
    launch.
-   Publish a privacy notice only after Labville approves wording that
    matches actual data handling.
