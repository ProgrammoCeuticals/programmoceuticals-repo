NAFDAC BIOEQUIVALENCE STUDY REPORT SUBMISSION CHECKLIST
General Instructions
Please submit this checklist together with the bioequivalence study report (including all appendices) and the bioequivalence trial information form (BTIF) for product screening and evaluation.
Provide/fill in as much detailed, accurate and final information as possible.
All the appended documents should be clearly identifiable by their location and tagging of the file names.
Kindly check that you have signed on the checklist, provided all requested information and enclosed all requested documents.
Should you have any queries regarding this procedure or the checklist, kindly contact Drug Evaluation and Research Directorate (DER) via e-mail der.headquarters@nafdac.gov.ng or registration@nafdac.gov.ng

*Reminder:
Please be informed that all data submitted to support the registration application for this product will be subjected to further evaluation.
Please refrain from changing/removing all submitted data unless requested by NAFDAC or the data has been updated as per latest registration requirements.
Kindly be reminded that decision whether the dossier is allowed for registration will be subjected to full evaluation and the final decision by NAFDAC.
